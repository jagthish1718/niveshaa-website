Questions

Before you ask

Does Niveshaa give buy/sell signals or investment advice?+

No. Niveshaa teaches concepts and lets you practice with a paper trading simulator using virtual money. It is an educational platform, not a source of investment advice or trading signals.

Is real money involved anywhere?+

Only for your membership or book purchase, paid through Razorpay. The paper trading simulator itself uses virtual money — nothing you do inside it touches real markets.

Which languages are supported right now?+

English, Tamil and Hindi are fully available today, lesson-for-lesson. Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam are planned next.

When is the app launching?+

Niveshaa is in final testing ahead of its Google Play launch. The lesson books are already available — see the Books section above.

Can I cancel my membership?+

Yes — memberships run for the term you choose (monthly, 3, 6 or 12 months) and simply don't renew if you choose not to continue.

Trading and investing carry real financial risk. Niveshaa is an educational tool — lessons, a paper trading simulator using virtual money, and general market information. Nothing on this app or website is investment advice, and past or simulated performance does not guarantee future results.
