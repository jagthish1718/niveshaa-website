Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your data across the Niveshaa app and website.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Niveshaa ("we", "us", "our") is a stock market learning app. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Niveshaa app and website (niveshaa.co.in), why we collect it, and how you can control it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address and profile photo, when you sign in or create an account.
  • Learning & activity data — lesson progress, quiz scores and leaderboard rank, checklist progress, Paper Trading orders and positions, and any notes you add to your trading journal.
  • Payment information — when you buy a membership, book, or virtual-cash refill, payment is processed by Razorpay. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded and basic order details (amount, plan, date). We never see or store your card, UPI, or net-banking details — those are handled entirely by Razorpay and your bank/UPI app.
  • AI Coach conversations — questions you ask our AI Coach are sent to our AI provider to generate an answer, and may be retained to improve answers and show you your chat history.
  • Device & technical data — app version, device type, and basic crash/error logs, used only to fix bugs and keep the app working.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the app — show your lessons, track Paper Trading and your journal, calculate your quiz/leaderboard rank, and remember your checklist progress.
  • To process payments and activate the membership, book, or refill you purchased.
  • To answer your questions through the AI Coach.
  • To send you important updates about your account or purchase, such as a payment receipt.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Who we share data with

We share the minimum data necessary with the following service providers, only to run the app:

  • Razorpay — payment processing.
  • Supabase — secure sign-in and database hosting for your account and app data.
  • Google Gemini — to generate AI Coach answers.
  • Market-data providers — to show live/delayed stock prices, business news and IPO details inside the app (no personal data is sent to these providers).

We do not share your data with advertisers, and we do not run ads in the app.

4. Paper Trading is simulated

All "trades" placed inside Paper Trading use virtual, play money. No real money, shares, or securities are ever bought or sold through Paper Trading, and it does not connect to any stock exchange or broker. Nothing in the app is investment advice — Niveshaa is an educational product only.

5. Data retention

We keep your account and activity data as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and data deleted, write to us at support@niveshaa.co.in and we'll delete it, except where we're required to keep payment records for legal or tax reasons.

6. Your rights

You can ask us, at any time, to show you what data we hold about you, correct inaccurate data, or delete your account and data. Write to support@niveshaa.co.in for any of these.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access controls) to protect your data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

Niveshaa is intended for users aged 18 and above. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we'll remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email support@niveshaa.co.in.
